Welcome to Ai Media Recover and Cleaner. This Privacy Policy explains how Walid Aourir ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It
The core functionality of Ai Media Recover and Cleaner requires access to your device's photo library, video library, and local file storage.

Media & File Access: To identify duplicate photos, similar photos, large videos, and other media, the App requests permission to access your device's media library and files.
Local Processing: All scanning, analysis, and recovery processes are performed locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your personal photos, videos, or files to our servers or any third-party servers.
Deletion of Files: The App allows you to delete unwanted files to free up storage space. This action is irreversible once completed through the App and confirmed via the iOS system prompts. We do not keep backups of files you choose to delete.

2. Third-Party Services
While we do not collect personal media data, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

Advertising: The App includes third-party advertisements. These advertising networks may use identifiers (such as the Apple Identifier for Advertisers - IDFA) to serve personalized ads, provided you have given consent through the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
In-App Purchases: If you purchase a premium subscription, the transaction is processed securely by Apple. We do not have access to your credit card information or billing details. Apple provides us with anonymous purchase status data to unlock premium features.

3. Analytics and Crash Data
We may collect anonymized usage data and crash reports to help us improve the App's performance and stability. This data does not contain any personal information or access to your media files.

4. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.
